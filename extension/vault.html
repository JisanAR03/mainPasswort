Personal Vault

Use Personal Vault without the desktop app

In extension-only mode your vaults live in this browser and everything — creating, unlocking, editing, filling and Google Drive sync — happens here. The desktop app is not needed.

What you give up compared with the desktop app:

  • The master password is typed into the browser, and the unlocked vault is held in the browser's memory. A compromised browser, or a malicious copy of this extension, exposes the vault.
  • An unlock lasts up to 6 hours (or until the browser closes), not 5 minutes of idle time. Lock it when you step away, or turn on "lock when the screen locks".
  • The vault file lives in this browser's own storage. Removing the extension or clearing its data deletes it — keep Google Drive sync on, or export a copy.
  • Two third-party libraries (kdbxweb, hash-wasm) read and write the vault here.
