Create a vault

There is no way to recover a forgotten master password.

  • Your vault is kept, encrypted, in this browser. Removing the extension or clearing its data deletes it — connect Google Drive or export a copy.
  • While it is unlocked (up to 6 hours, or until the browser closes) the vault is held in the browser's memory: lock it when you step away.
